Who We Are

A foundation built on one simple belief — every life deserves a chance to flourish

Since our registration on 30th June 2020 (CAC/IT/NO 147769), David Life Touching Foundation has worked at the intersection of healthcare, education and welfare — reaching widows, students, orphans and young athletes across Imo State with practical, lasting support.

We don't just run programs; we build relationships with the communities we serve, returning again and again until the change we make is one that lasts.

  • Accessible HealthcarePartnering with medical centres to reach the underserved
  • Educational OpportunityScholarships, quiz competitions and school equipment
  • Community WelfareDirect outreach to widows, orphans and vulnerable families

Our Story

A mission-driven organisation dedicated to lasting social change

David Life Touching Foundation focuses on providing accessible healthcare, enhancing educational opportunities and supporting the welfare of vulnerable communities. Through outreach programs, partnerships and sustainable initiatives, we aim to create lasting social change and empower individuals for a better future.

What began as a personal conviction to give back has grown into a structured foundation with five active programs, dozens of partner schools and hundreds of families reached — all guided by one founder's belief that every life touched sends ripples far beyond what we can see.

Our Vision

A society where every individual — regardless of background — has access to healthcare, education and opportunity, and where communities are empowered to sustain their own progress long after our outreach ends.

Our Mission

To improve the wellbeing of society by positively impacting lives — providing accessible healthcare, enhancing educational opportunities, and supporting the welfare of vulnerable communities through sustainable, community-led initiatives.
